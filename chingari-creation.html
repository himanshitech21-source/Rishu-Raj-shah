FAQ

Questions we get asked most.

How long does a typical website take?+

A landing page usually ships in 3–5 days. A full business website or e-commerce build takes 2–4 weeks depending on scope and content readiness.

Do you work with businesses outside your city?+

Yes — we deliver pan-India, remotely for most services, and travel on-site for video and drone shoots when needed.

Can FlowNex connect to my existing WhatsApp number?+

Yes, FlowNex integrates with your existing WhatsApp Business number via the official API — no need to switch numbers.

Do you offer support after the project is delivered?+

Every project includes a support window post-launch, and ongoing maintenance plans are available after that for updates and fixes.

What's included in the starting price?+

Starting prices cover the core build as listed. Add-ons like extra pages, custom integrations or rush timelines are quoted separately after a quick discovery call.

Can you handle both the website and the marketing?+

Yes — in collaboration with Circle Creation we run Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO and branding alongside development, so the site and the marketing are built to work together.
